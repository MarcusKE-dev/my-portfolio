Web developer in Kenya

I am John Mwaura, known as Dev John, an independent web and software developer in Kenya. I build business websites, M-Pesa online stores, custom software and WhatsApp workflows. Website starting prices begin at KSh 10,000 or $100, and I reply within 24 hours.

A useful website for a local business

A customer looking for a plumber, a clinic or a place to eat needs clear information before making contact. I build pages around those questions: what the business does, which service fits and how to send an enquiry. A website can give that information a stable home alongside your social profiles and conversations.

For a small business, a large platform may be unnecessary. A landing page can present one focused offer. A site with separate service pages gives more room to explain the work. The right starting package depends on the content and the actions customers need, rather than the largest list of features.

KSh starting prices and a clear scope

Landing Page starts at KSh 10,000 or $100. Basic Website starts at KSh 15,000 or $150 for up to 3 pages. Business Website starts at KSh 20,000 or $200 for up to 5 pages, while Professional Website starts at KSh 30,000 or $300 for up to 8 pages.

An online store starts at KSh 35,000 or $350. Smaller custom software starts at KSh 40,000 or $400, and advanced platforms use a custom quote. Every figure is a starting point. The complete KSh and USD pricing guide lists the package scope so we can discuss the right fit before agreeing a final price.

M-Pesa and WhatsApp in the customer journey

For a Kenyan business, the path from a product enquiry to an order may involve both WhatsApp and M-Pesa. A website can make contact easy, while a shop can put the catalogue, checkout and order records together. I work with M-Pesa Daraja for the payment connection agreed in the project scope.

A WhatsApp button opens a conversation. A WhatsApp automation workflow goes further by capturing records, sending notifications or connecting the conversation to business tools. Those are different scopes. Describe the manual work you want to reduce so we can identify what needs to be built.

The e-commerce development guide explains the starter store and its payment boundaries. If the main need is a staff tool rather than a public shop, the custom software service is a better starting discussion.

Phones, data use and local search

A business site needs to work well for a customer opening it on a phone. I consider readable text, practical navigation and images that do not make the page unnecessarily heavy. A short route to WhatsApp, a call or an enquiry form helps someone act when they have found the right service.

Local search starts with accurate information. The service descriptions, business identity and relevant location details should agree with the rest of the business presence. The website packages list basic SEO setup. I also offer performance engineering and technical SEO, without promising rankings or a fixed number of enquiries.
