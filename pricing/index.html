What changes the final price

Page count affects how much information needs a layout and how many pages need review. A single focused page has a different scope from a site with separate pages for several services. More custom design also means more decisions about the structure, content and presentation.

Integrations such as M-Pesa or Stripe bring payment flows into the project. The required connection, the provider access and the behaviour after a payment all need to be described. An admin area can range from a small set of forms to several staff roles and detailed reports.

For a shop, the number of products and the information attached to each product affect the work. Product variations, stock handling and order workflows should be discussed before treating them as included. The starter package lists a catalogue, categories, cart, checkout, order management and basic store administration.

Content needs are part of the conversation too. Tell me whether the wording, product details and photos are ready, or whether writing and photography still need to be arranged. Let me know your intended deadline so we can discuss it with the rest of the scope. No delivery time is promised by these package prices.

How a project runs

  1. Message me with the business goal and the kind of website or software you need.
  2. We discuss the requirements and agree what the project covers.
  3. You get a written quote for the agreed scope.
  4. I build the website or application around those requirements.
  5. You review the work and we discuss it against the scope.
  6. The project moves to launch.

A useful first message explains who will use the result and what they should be able to do. If you already have a site, include its address and explain the part that needs attention. This gives the discussion more substance than choosing a package from the price alone.

Pricing questions

What is the lowest starting price?

The Landing Page package starts at KSh 10,000 or $100. It covers one responsive page, WhatsApp and click-to-call buttons, a contact or enquiry form, basic on-page SEO and clear calls to action.

How much does a small business website cost?

Basic Website starts at KSh 15,000 or $150 for up to 3 pages. Business Website starts at KSh 20,000 or $200 for up to 5 pages. The final choice depends on the information and functionality you need.

What does an online store start at?

E-commerce Website starts at KSh 35,000 or $350. The exact payment integration and store requirements are agreed per project.

Is custom software always KSh 40,000 or $400?

No. That is a starting price for smaller, clearly scoped projects. Marketplaces, SaaS, booking platforms, complex API work and other advanced products need a custom quote.

Is a product catalogue the same as a shop?

A catalogue showcase presents products or services. A shop also needs a cart, checkout and order handling. The Professional Website package lists a showcase; the E-commerce Website package lists those shop features.

Can M-Pesa or Stripe change the scope?

Yes. Payment integration needs to match the checkout and business workflow. Tell me which payment flow you need so the connection can be included in the agreed quote.

Can I discuss the budget before choosing a package?

Yes. Send the business goal, required features and your budget through WhatsApp, email or the project form. I reply within 24 hours.

Are these final fixed prices?

No. Every figure is a starting price. The final price depends on the functionality and scope agreed, and the exact amount is confirmed in writing.

Send a short description of the business, the people who will use the website or software, and the part you need help with. I reply within 24 hours.
