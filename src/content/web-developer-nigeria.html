Two hours apart, with room for discussion

Nigeria uses West Africa Time, UTC+1. Kenya uses East Africa Time, UTC+3, without daylight saving. When it is 09:00 in Nigeria, it is 11:00 in Kenya. Comparing a normal 09:00 to 17:00 day in both places gives six shared hours, from 09:00 to 15:00 in Nigeria.
