Give visitors the information they came for

A business website should explain what you do, who the service is for and how to get in touch. A clinic may need separate service information and a clear contact route. A restaurant needs a useful presentation of its food and business details. A tradesperson needs to show the work and make an enquiry easy.

I start with those visitor tasks rather than a long list of effects. Clear headings, readable text and a practical mobile layout help someone understand the business. A WhatsApp button or enquiry form gives them a next step, with the exact contact features determined by the package.

Website packages and their boundaries

Landing Page starts at KSh 10,000 or $100 for one responsive page. It includes WhatsApp and click-to-call buttons, a contact or enquiry form, basic on-page SEO and clear calls to action. It suits a focused service, product or campaign that does not need several separate pages.

Basic Website starts at KSh 15,000 or $150 for up to 3 pages. The package covers a responsive layout, business and service information, contact details, WhatsApp, a contact form and basic SEO. Business Website starts at KSh 20,000 or $200 for up to 5 pages, adding customised business-focused design, about, services and contact sections, with social and location links where appropriate.

Professional Website starts at KSh 30,000 or $300 for up to 8 pages. It allows more detailed layouts, expanded information and a product or service catalogue showcase. A showcase is different from a store with a cart and checkout. For online ordering through a shop, read about e-commerce website development.

Mobile speed and local search

People may open a business site on a phone using a limited data connection. I consider image size, page structure and how much the browser needs to load. Contact controls should be easy to reach, and a visitor should not have to zoom into tiny text to understand a service.

Basic on-page SEO includes making page topics understandable through titles, descriptions, headings and content. Relevant business and location information should be accurate. Technical SEO can support search discovery, but no website package guarantees a Google position. Existing sites may also need a separate performance or technical review.
