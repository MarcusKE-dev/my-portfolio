Frequently asked questions

I am John Mwaura, known as Dev John, an independent web and software developer in Kenya. These answers cover my work, starting prices and how to contact me. For a project-specific answer, send me the requirements.

About me

Who is Dev John?

Dev John is my working name. I am John Mwaura, an independent full-stack, web and software developer in Kenya, working since 2023. I build business websites, stores, custom software and automation.

Is Dev John the same as John Mwaura?

Yes. Dev John and Dev.John refer to me, John Mwaura, the web and software developer behind devjohn.site. My public profiles are linked on this site.

What is ABC Systems and what is your link to it?

I am the founder of ABC Systems, a Nairobi technology company with the line “Anything Business Computing”. The company covers Build, Grow, Automate and Equip, and runs an online store and ABC Learn.

What is UWAZI?

UWAZI is my flagship public finance transparency platform for Kenya. It makes government expenditure searchable. The project uses React, TypeScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL and Supabase.

Pricing

How much does a website cost in Kenya?

My Landing Page package starts at KSh 10,000 or $100. Basic Website starts at KSh 15,000 or $150, Business Website at KSh 20,000 or $200, and Professional Website at KSh 30,000 or $300. Final pricing depends on the agreed scope.

How much does an e-commerce website cost?

The E-commerce Website package starts at KSh 35,000 or $350. It includes a catalogue, categories, cart, checkout, order management, basic store administration and payment integration appropriate to the scope.

Do you build custom software?

Yes. Smaller, clearly scoped software projects start at KSh 40,000 or $400. Larger platforms, marketplaces, SaaS, booking products and complex integrations need a custom quote.

Yes. I can serve Uganda, Tanzania, Nigeria, the USA, Canada and the UK remotely. We can discuss the project by email, WhatsApp or a call, then agree the scope and quote in writing.

Technical

Yes. I use the WhatsApp Cloud API for lead capture, order workflows, notifications and connections to existing business tools. The scope determines whether a smaller custom software package or a custom quote fits.
