Working with international clients

I am John Mwaura, known as Dev John, a web and software developer in Kenya. I can work remotely with clients in the USA, Canada and the UK, with starting prices shown in USD and KSh. We agree the scope and quote in writing before the build.

How we can run the project remotely

Start with a message about the business, the people who will use the result and the task it needs to support. An existing website address, a service list or an example workflow helps make the first discussion concrete. You can send this by email, WhatsApp or the project enquiry form.

We can discuss the details in a scoping call or chat, then agree the scope and a written quote in USD. During the build, updates and review keep the work connected to those requirements. We discuss the result before launch. The communication method can be email, WhatsApp or a call on the platform you prefer.

Remote work benefits from written questions that include enough context to answer. For a page review, name the page and explain the change. For an application, describe the action and expected result. This helps us use the time difference productively without assuming a fixed meeting or update schedule.

Time zones and honest overlap

Kenya stays on East Africa Time, UTC+3, all year. The US, Canada and UK locations shown below change their clocks in summer. The graphic compares an illustrative 09:00 to 17:00 working day in each location, rather than promising that I am available during every displayed hour.

The dates used for the comparison are January and July 2026. The table gives the same information as the graphic in plain text. The USA and Canada have several time zones, so tell me your city or zone when proposing a call.

For clients in the USA

Eastern mornings reach the end of the illustrative Kenyan working day. In summer, a 09:00 Eastern start gives one hour of shared working time. In winter, the two normal working days meet at a boundary and have no shared interval. Pacific working hours have no overlap with that Kenyan 09:00 to 17:00 example in either season.

A US project should therefore make good use of written scope and review notes. If live discussion is needed, we agree a suitable time rather than assume all American mornings overlap. A business website, online shop or internal tool can all be discussed remotely, with the required functionality determining the quote.

For clients in Canada

Toronto is the Eastern example in the table. It has the same seasonal offsets as New York, giving one hour of shared normal working time in summer and none in winter. Other Canadian locations need their own time zone check. Including your zone in the first message avoids an ambiguous meeting time.

For a Canadian company considering custom software, describe the forms, records and review steps that staff use now. I can discuss a dashboard or data workflow against that process. A small scoped application and a larger platform belong in different pricing categories, regardless of where the client is based.

For clients in the UK

The UK has five shared hours with Kenya in winter and six in summer when comparing normal 09:00 to 17:00 working days. That leaves a practical window for a scope discussion or page review during the UK day. Kenya itself does not move its clocks.

If the project is a service website, bring the services, intended audience and available content. For a product catalogue or shop, explain whether customers will only enquire or will place orders through checkout. This distinction helps choose between a website package and the e-commerce scope before talking about design details.

A practical choice based on work and scope

I work in English, publish starting prices in dollars and show real projects you can inspect. East Africa Time gives a useful overlap with Europe, while North American projects need more deliberate scheduling and written communication. Those are practical planning facts, without a claim that every location shares the same working day.

My website development, online store development and custom software work are described separately so you can compare the scope. For customer message workflows, there is also a WhatsApp automation guide.

Prices and what to prepare

A landing page starts at $100 or KSh 10,000. Business Website starts at $200 or KSh 20,000, E-commerce Website at $350 or KSh 35,000, and smaller custom software at $400 or KSh 40,000. These are starting points; advanced products need a custom quote and the final amount depends on the agreed scope.

Read the full pricing guide, then gather the business description, required pages or workflows, existing website and content status. Include a budget and intended deadline if you have them. No deposit terms, receiving payment provider or delivery duration is assumed by the published starting prices.
