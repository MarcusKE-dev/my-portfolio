E-commerce website development

I build online stores with a product catalogue, categories, cart, checkout and order handling. The E-commerce Website package starts at KSh 35,000 or $350. I am John Mwaura, known as Dev John, and I agree the payment setup and final scope with each business.

What the starter store includes

The starter package gives customers a way to browse products and submit an order. Product categories and detail pages organise the catalogue. A cart and checkout connect the browsing experience to the information the business needs to handle the order.

The package lists order submission and management, basic store administration and a responsive design. Payment integration is appropriate to the agreed scope. The published starting price does not mean every possible retail feature is included. A detailed stock system or a complex operational dashboard needs a separate discussion.

M-Pesa Daraja and Stripe

M-Pesa Daraja and Stripe are part of my integration stack. A payment connection needs to fit the checkout and the way an order is recorded. The project discussion should identify the required payment flow, the provider access and what the store should do after the payment status changes.

I agree the exact payment setup per project. Before treating any provider as part of a quote, we need to understand the business account and the available API access. The payment functionality listed for one existing project is an example of that project, rather than a universal promise for every new store.

Inventory, orders and the admin area

A customer-facing catalogue and an internal inventory system answer different questions. The catalogue shows what someone can buy. Staff may also need stock records, purchase tracking or reports. Tell me which of these processes already have a tool and which ones need to be built.

Basic store administration is included in the starter package. The required fields, order handling and product information should be agreed before development. If several staff roles, deeper inventory rules or connections to other business software are needed, those requirements affect the scope.

A useful way to prepare is to describe one order from start to finish. What does the customer choose? Which details do they provide? Who sees the order in the business, and what needs to happen next? This makes the required screens and data easier to identify without assuming extra features.

What changes the price of a store

The catalogue size, the information attached to products and the amount of custom presentation all affect the work. Variations, inventory behaviour and the required order workflow need to be described. Integrations and admin features can be more significant than the number of public pages.

Bring a sample of your product information, the categories you expect and an explanation of how orders are currently handled. Mention whether descriptions and photographs are ready. If you have an existing shop or stock system, include its address or name so we can discuss the connection rather than assume it exists.

Online store questions

How much does an e-commerce website start at?

The starting price is KSh 35,000 or $350. Final pricing depends on the store functionality and scope agreed for the project.

Is every payment method included?

No. The package lists payment integration appropriate to the agreed project scope. I discuss the exact M-Pesa, Stripe or other required setup before quoting.

Can I start with a catalogue instead of checkout?

A product or service catalogue showcase is listed in the Professional Website package. That may fit a business that needs to present items and receive enquiries rather than take cart-based orders.

What if I need a larger commerce platform?

Multi-vendor marketplaces and larger custom products need a custom quote. Explain the seller, customer and admin workflows so they can be scoped.
