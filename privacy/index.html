Privacy policy

This page explains how devjohn.site handles information sent through the project form and contact links. I use the information you send to read and reply to your enquiry. You can contact me by email to ask for deletion.

Information in the project form

The form asks for your name, email, phone number, company, service, budget, timeline and project message. The company field is optional. Please include the project details needed for an enquiry and avoid sending passwords or other access credentials.

The form is sent through Web3Forms. That service processes the submission so it can be delivered to me. Its privacy policy describes its own handling of submitted information.

WhatsApp, email and external links

WhatsApp links open WhatsApp, sometimes with a draft message you can edit before sending. Email links open your email application. Those services handle the messages you send through them under their own policies.

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, live projects and ABC Systems take you to other websites. The contact form does not submit when you simply follow one of those links.

Browser storage and resources

The site stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser using local storage. You can remove it through your browser settings. The site does not include an analytics script or a tracking cookie banner.

Page fonts load from Google Fonts. Technology icons on the homepage and About page load from a public CDN. These resource requests go to those providers when your browser loads the resources.

Deletion requests

Email mwaura.ke.john@gmail.com if you want to ask about or request deletion of information you sent to me. Include enough context to identify the enquiry. Do not send additional sensitive details for this purpose.

Tell me what you want to build

Send a short description of the business, the people who will use the website or software, and the part you need help with. I reply within 24 hours.
