About Me

Building software that solves real problems.

I'm an independent Full-Stack Developer focused on designing and building production-grade web applications, business automation systems, and digital platforms that help organisations operate more efficiently.

Since 2023, I have worked across e-commerce, automation, public-sector technology, and business operations software. My work spans the entire product lifecycle from planning and architecture to deployment, monitoring, and long-term maintenance.

My flagship project, UWAZI, is a public finance transparency platform designed to make government expenditure more accessible and accountable. Alongside civic-tech initiatives, I build commercial systems including payment platforms, administrative dashboards, customer automation tools, and business websites.

I believe good software should be reliable, secure, maintainable, and built around the people who use it. Every project I take on is approached with a long-term engineering mindset rather than a quick-delivery mentality.
